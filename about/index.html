About Me

Rajarao Pedini

Software QA Engineer · Hyderabad

I'm a Software QA Engineer based in Hyderabad, currently leading the QA department at Vanix Technologies. My work spans web apps, mobile apps, APIs, servers and databases, and my job is simple to state and hard to do well: make sure nothing reaches users until it's genuinely ready.

Get in touch

Leading QA at Vanix

At Vanix, QA goes beyond software. Alongside software testing, firmware and hardware QA also run under my mentorship. I set up the QA department and its structure from the ground up, and I train and mentor the interns on my team.

I own quality at the company level: builds must be properly validated before they reach QA, defects are tracked end to end, and I prepare project-wise developer and QA performance reports. These reports help trace the root cause whenever a deadline slips, so the team can act early, prevent repeat misses and keep every project on schedule.

What my testing covers

My work covers every kind of audit a product needs: functional testing, performance testing for stability and scalability, and security testing. I also work hands-on with AI agents and modern testing tools, bringing them in wherever they make testing faster, deeper or more reliable.

Where it started

Before Vanix, I worked for about a year as a Junior QA Analyst, plus 6–8 months of dedicated training and hands-on work. That period built my foundation: I studied what makes a QA structure work, its strengths and its gaps, and learned the best practices I now apply while leading my own department.
